# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want open-fusion-mcp to be a welcoming place to learn, build and disagree productively. Everyone who takes
part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space, is expected to treat others with
respect, whatever their experience, background, identity or tools of choice.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique work, not people.
- Be specific and constructive, especially when a change is not ready.
- Respect people's time: include reproductions, versions and what you already tried.
- Credit others' work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public. Report problems to
the maintainers at legionsoundofficial@gmail.com. Reports are handled promptly and confidentially. Maintainers
may remove, edit or reject contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban
anyone who does, in proportion to the harm.
