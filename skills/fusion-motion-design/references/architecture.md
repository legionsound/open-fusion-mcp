# Architecture and original art direction

## Brief to visual idea

Start with the audience and the one thing they should understand or do.
A prompt, image, logo, brand assets or project context is enough to begin;
do not require a video to imitate. Inspect supplied assets and confirm their
roles. Distinguish literal product behavior, explanatory abstraction and
aspirational concept. Keep that distinction in delivery notes.

Choose a visual mechanism that communicates the idea: a type transformation,
reveal, spatial relationship, diagram, object, cut, accumulation, comparison,
or interaction. A device mockup is one option, not the default for every brief.
A title, broadcast package, identity, data explanation and film graphic need
different staging. Do not repeat one card layout under changing headings.

## Compact design passport

Record only decisions that affect construction or acceptance:

- Format: resolution, pixel aspect, frame rate, duration, color assumptions,
  delivery context and audio scope.
- Message: main idea, copy hierarchy, required facts, concept boundaries.
- Visual system: type roles/weights, palette roles, contrast, spacing,
  alignment, radius/stroke logic, depth/material treatment and safe areas.
- Components: semantic names, bounds, anchors, contents, connections and assets.
- Beats: purpose, focus, entrance, settle, hold, exit and transitions.
- Controls: editable text/colors/assets, transform/timing controls, defaults.
- Evidence: representative poses, risky in-betweens, control edits, save/export
  and delivery checks.

## Craft before node count

Design a convincing settled frame first. Use optical alignment, negative space
and selective asymmetry. Check type at actual delivery size. Keep body copy
short enough to read during its hold; required reading time depends on copy,
audience and competing motion. Do not borrow fixed UI pixel tokens as film laws.

Assign one dominant action per beat. Supporting movement follows or prepares
that action. Carry recognizable elements through transitions so change has a
cause. Position, scale, crop, depth, masks and cuts can do work that another
fade cannot. Use stillness to let the idea land.

Use different easing for different jobs. A sharp response, deliberate glide,
mechanical move and elastic accent need not share a house curve. Linear motion
is valid for constant-speed travel or a playhead. Add overshoot only where its
weight and meaning fit. No compulsory wiggle, bounce, centered headings or
universal reveal preset.

Inspect the overlap between outgoing and incoming shots. Two good poses can
produce unreadable intervening typography or an object crossing new copy.
Retune choreography before decorating it.

## Construction architecture

The passport describes intent; the Fusion graph is the editable result.
Use native Text+, masks, Backgrounds, explicit Merge/Transform chains and
qualified effects. Keep component controls separate from internal plumbing.
FlowView positions organize nodes; they are not image coordinates.

Use real 3D when changing perspective, thickness, lighting or occlusion matters.
Use 2D/2.5D when it communicates the same thing more simply. Photographic assets
and imported geometry may remain external, with source/provenance retained.
Do not bake editable wording or chrome to avoid graph work.

Native graphs are the default. Package a macro/template only when requested
and after its controls and round trip are qualified. Existing interchange
animations have separate limits in [format bridges](html-ograf-lottie.md).
