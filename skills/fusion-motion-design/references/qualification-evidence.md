# Qualification evidence and historical boundaries

Observed session: 2026-09-17, Resolve Studio 21.1.0.14 on macOS. These are bounded
observations, not a portable certification. Read current host documentation
before applying the recipes elsewhere.

## Source evidence

The session's working files (capability matrix, final QC receipt, delivery notes,
recovery checkpoint, design passport, final composition and project export) belong
to a client project and are not part of this repository. They are historical
examples, not required inputs for new work. If evidence is unavailable, say so; do
not invent equivalent evidence.

## What was established

Native plate/TextPlus/mask/Merge construction; explicit transforms/controllers;
scalar spline animation with exact replacement and relative Python handles;
authored rounded OBJ meshes, classic camera/lights/renderer and editable image
plane screen; still-image holds; PNG Saver and encoded H.264 output.

The concept was designed from a text brief, without a reference video. Final cut:
1920x1080, 24 fps, 576 frames, 24 seconds, silent. The encoded result was inspected
at 3 fps across its length (earlier revised transitions at 6 fps) and decoded in
full without errors. Closing text, color and group scale were changed, rendered,
inspected and restored. Timing changes were inspected in revised transitions; a
published timing-control UI was not qualified on its own.

A saved 322-tool intermediate graph survived one approved Resolve restart. The final
386-tool graph was saved and exported, but was not reopened as a separate
persistence test. The user, not the agent, restored the RGB viewer.

The artwork shows a product direction. The illustrated agent workflow was not yet
shipped, so it is not proof of app behavior.

## Genuine remaining qualification

- Scrollable viewport replacement tests; Text+ followers/per-character motion.
- Macro/UserControls publishing, Edit-page usability, .setting import round trip.
- OGraf/Lottie import, alpha/fonts/timing and persistence.
- USDZ or broad USD/FBX/3D material support; motion-blur/particle/audio modules.
- Universal API/UI or AE/Fusion parity; arbitrary visual-style mastery.
- Smaller-model execution and uninterrupted 1x/user creative acceptance.

Next optional test, only when requested: one disposable original title or diagram on
the documented native path, with a published timing control, a content edit and an
